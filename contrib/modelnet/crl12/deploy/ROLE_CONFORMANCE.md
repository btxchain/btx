# Role conformance and operator boundary

Every role declaration binds an enrolled identity, network, exact operation registry and actual evidence. A signature is not customer authority or independent certification.

DISCOVERY: original authorship, bounded search, exact package bytes, source outage and no wallet dependency.
CUSTODY: actual native keys/scripts, funds, signer ambiguity, backup and recovery; inherit HCP evidence.
EXECUTION: actual eligible venue operations, quotes, idempotency and partial outcomes; inherit HCP/v1.1.
FUNDING: native release/bounty/claim/refund and per-account mapping; inherit HCP/v1.1.
TREASURY: real reserve availability, floor, mandates and concurrent allocation; inherit v1.1.
DEVICE_HANDOFF: owner-local verification, grant, acquisition and real runtime readiness; inherit JIT/HCP.
ASSET_SERVICING: asset/rights/source identity, temporal position data, valuation and reconciliation.
PORTFOLIO_ANALYTICS: scoped metrics, snapshots, direct/look-through, draft-only instructions and scenarios.
FIAT_RAIL: actual operator-approved fiat service and its permitted flow; not presumed digital-asset support.

A provider may claim only a subset. The full v1.2 implementation delivers all generic interfaces and tests; an operator advertises its actually supported deployment. New and incumbent providers use the same process.
