## 8. ROOT CAUSE OF THE DISCOVERY FAILURE (found 2026-08-28)

Commit `3ebe76c9 doc: compiled seeds disconnect consensus nodes for missing NODE_NETWORK`.

`matmulvalidation=relay` **strips NODE_NETWORK** from the seeds by design (they are
pointers, not block sources). But peer-retention logic **disconnects peers lacking
NODE_NETWORK** unless the connection is addr-fetch. So the compiled-in seeds are dropped
by the very nodes trying to bootstrap through them.

This explains, with one cause:
- 157 GETADDR received vs 30 answered — connections do not survive
- addrman entirely ~13.97 days old — nothing new is ever learned
- 45 tip-class peers connected but not retained

Same structural error as every other defect in this line: **0.34 made seeds relay-only by
design, but retention still required the service bit relay mode removes.** A capability
silently disabled for exactly the configuration we ship.

Fix in progress: `HandshakeKeepsDiscoveryPeer` now keeps NODE_MATMUL_DISCOVERY peers even
when the connection is not addr-fetch.

Acceptance: fresh datadir, seeds only, no addnode, must reach the live chain.
