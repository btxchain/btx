# Economic defense without a privileged dealer

The defense is optional intermediaries, not a promise that BTX never leaves holders' wallets. Direct native BTC/ETH settlement removes mandatory exchange custody and exchange-token conversion. It does not prevent unfavorable voluntary prices, external selling, Sybil providers, market-maker concentration or a counterparty declining to fund.

The candidate adds competitive quote comparison, optional owner-selected price references, conservative fee/size controls, inventory-aware maker policies, visible local outcomes and bounded noncustodial authorization. Providers retain their own inventory. Users retain the ability to decline, select another maker or use an outside venue.

Do not impose an official BTX price floor, pretend that a signature proves independent ownership, or create a central reserve pool to solve concentration. Any compensated atomic-swap or performance-bond design needs a separate protocol: identify the compensated loss, enforceable fault condition, collateral custody, cancellation timing, adversarial abort incentive, and effect of reorgs on both chains. This package does not label a research-only penalty design as an implemented protection.

Maker inventory reservations are local promises. A verified transaction commits real assets only after chain validation. Proof of an address balance is not proof of exclusive reserves or of total solvency. Reference-price feeds are selected by the owner, not appointed by the protocol. A configurable quorum of endpoints remains a trust assumption unless backed by actual chain verification.
